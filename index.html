Registered players by age group

Players are grouped by the section they registered for on chess-results.com. Boys play the open sections (U8 to U18) and girls play the girls sections (G8 to G18). A girl listed under an open section is shown in the girls group of the same age and marked with the section she was listed as. Pick a cell to see that group.
